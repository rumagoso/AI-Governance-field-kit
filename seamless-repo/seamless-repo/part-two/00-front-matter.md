# Part Two: Field Manual

*A note before the first chapter*

The eight chapters that follow each pair with an essay from Part One, and each hands you something to actually fill in: a record, a worksheet, an inventory, a checklist. None of them are meant to be exhaustive.

Adopt each one as written for the first few systems you apply it to, and it will start telling you what it's missing for your own environment, an extra field, a different category, a step your organisation's actual workflow needs that a generic template couldn't anticipate. That's expected. A template that survives contact with real use completely unmodified probably wasn't specific enough to begin with.

One rule is worth keeping even as every chapter's form evolves in your hands: any field that records a status gets a named person attached to it too. A record where every box is ticked and no name appears anywhere isn't evidence that something was done well. It's evidence nobody was asked to own it, which is the exact failure this whole book is about.

This is also why the templates live here, on GitHub, rather than printed once and left still. Update your own copies as your environment changes. If you build something better than what's here, the door's open to send it back.
