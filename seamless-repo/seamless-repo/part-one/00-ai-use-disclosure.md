# A note on process

This book was drafted with the assistance of a large language model, used for iteration on structure and phrasing, editing passes, and research support in verifying the regulations, cases, and incidents cited throughout. The arguments, the judgment calls about what to include and what to leave out, and the decisions about what the evidence actually means are the author's own.

Every real incident, case, and regulatory citation in this book has been checked against a primary or authoritative secondary source at the time of writing. Given how quickly this field moves, some of those facts will have changed by the time you're reading this; where that matters to an essay's argument rather than just its illustration, Part Two's live repository is where corrections and updates will appear.

Readers are told this plainly for the same reason this book keeps asking everyone else to name who did what: a disclosure left off the page is one more part nobody owned.
